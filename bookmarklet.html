TLDV Transcript Bookmarklet

Collect the full transcript of a TLDV meeting in one click, no copying HTML

Loading…

Install:

  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B)
  2. Drag the blue button above onto the bookmarks bar

Use:

  1. Open the meeting on tldv.io with the transcript visible
  2. Click the bookmark and wait; the transcript scrolls by itself while a panel shows progress
  3. When it finishes, click "Copy" or "Download .txt" in the panel

Why this is needed: TLDV only fills in the transcript paragraphs that are near the visible area, so copying the HTML of a long meeting only gets a small part of it. The bookmarklet scrolls through every paragraph and collects the text. It runs only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Source: bookmarklet.js.
